Dancing Mouse Jiggler — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27

Dancing Mouse Jiggler (the "app", published by Michael Mu on the Microsoft Store) does not collect, store, share or transmit any personal information.

  • The app has no network access and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • It keeps one small file on your own PC, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\DancingMouse: a short activity log (log.txt) with the times of the actions it took. The file never leaves your PC. It may remain after you uninstall the app; you can delete that folder yourself at any time.
  • To do its job, the app sends a keyboard event (Scroll Lock, pressed twice) to Windows and checks how long the keyboard and mouse have been idle. This is used on the spot and is not recorded beyond the log lines described above.
  • The app does not read, record or send what you type or what is on your screen.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/MichaelMuClaude/dancing-mouse-privacy/issues.
